# Preparation-first design reset: R2

R2 adds the large preparation header state within the red R1 band. The earlier detailed Home overview remains temporarily; R3 replaces it with the selected compact course/evidence summary. R4 reorganizes Settings. PR 8 and its audio branch remain untouched.

This is a product indicator, not an empirically calibrated probability of passing, a guarantee of readiness or permanent mastery. Labels never say Ready to pass. Values come from the existing shared dashboard, not illustrative 42% or 78% examples.

Formula when eligible: 50% course progression + 30% recent objective practice + 20% recent timed mock performance. Course progression is the arithmetic mean of the completed-lesson ratio and completed-chapter ratio, giving both validated chapter requirements and lesson progress a role. Question-free studied exceptions count as course progress, not assessed mastery. Practice uses the existing latest eligible objective outcome per distinct concept over 30 days. Mocks use the mean of up to five individual score ratios, not raw correct-count pooling.

The combined percentage requires valid course denominators, at least five assessed practice concepts and two eligible timed mock attempts in 30 days. These minimum sample rules are explicit product heuristics, not scientific confidence thresholds. A familiar-bank score is not independent predictive evidence. No high-confidence or passing-probability classification is introduced.

Empty history shows a dash and Indicators are being established. Course-only activity shows Preparation in progress plus the explicitly labelled course percentage, with no combined score. Missing/limited performance shows Insufficient recent evidence and a dash. Missing components are not zeroed or renormalized. A genuinely assessed zero can display zero only when the full eligibility rules are met.

Tapping the header opens a bilingual explanation with weights, actual component percentages, lesson/chapter counts, concept sample count, mock count/latest date, eligibility rules, exclusions and a View progress link. Reading time, listening, bookmarks, streaks and exam countdown are excluded. The headline model does not write to progress storage or change completion, quiz scoring, SRS, recommendations, reminders or migrations.

Eleven model tests are supplied but not executed. No tests, lint, typecheck, native build or before/after verification were run, as requested. Device/CI acceptance is unconfirmed. The Home is still an intermediate R2 design until R3-R4 land.
